Tips

Tips without friction.

Every step between a fan wanting to tip and the tip actually landing costs the creator money. Friction doesn't slow payments down — it kills them.

In the old way, a fan watches your video. Wants to tip. Sees a phone-number form and a multi-step process — and drops the tip entirely. The fan wanted to pay; the flow talked them out of it.

CreaPay's tip flow is the shortest thing that still works: scan the QR on the video — or tap the link in your bio — pay, done. No phone number, no account, no forms. From a fan in your city or a fan on another continent — the money just arrives. The tip that starts is the tip that lands.

Your income stays private. Fans see the tip — never how much you make from anyone else, anywhere.
